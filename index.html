About ElectroPanel Industries

Engineering Excellence in Electrical Solutions

Established in 1995, ElectroPanel Industries has been at the forefront of electrical panel manufacturing in India. With over 25 years of experience, we have successfully delivered innovative and reliable electrical solutions to various industries including manufacturing, infrastructure, renewable energy, and commercial sectors.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility spans over 50,000 sq ft and is equipped with modern machinery, advanced testing equipment, and a skilled workforce of 200+ professionals. We are committed to delivering world-class products that meet international quality standards.
